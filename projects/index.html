Projects

Projects where I used data cleaning, visualization, and predictive modeling to answer practical questions.

  • January 2026–March 2026

    Sports Supplement Popularity Analysis

    Analyzed Amazon sports nutrition data to understand how price, ratings, brand, and category relate to product popularity. After engineering price-per-serving and log-review features, I built a linear regression model that explained 52.8% of the variation in review-based popularity.

    Tools: Python, pandas, and Jupyter Notebook

  • September 2025–December 2025

    Recipe Nutrition Analysis

    Studied 83,782 recipes and 731,927 user interactions to identify features associated with healthier nutrition profiles. A tuned random forest improved protein prediction from an R² of 0.50 to 0.77.

    Tools: Python, pandas, scikit-learn, and Jupyter Notebook
